⏰ 08:15 AM 📍 University Union & Dexter Lawn

Morning Coffee & Walk Across Campus

The morning fog was just starting to lift over the St. Lucia Mountains as I walked from my apartment down Grand Avenue. My first stop on campus is always Scout Coffee in the UU. I grabbed a hot honey lavender latte and a buttermilk biscuit before heading across Dexter Lawn toward my 9:00 AM lecture.

⏰ 10:10 AM 📍 Bonderson Projects Center

Building Circuits: "Learn by Doing" in Action

Directly after lecture, I headed into the Bonderson Engineering Building for my microcontroller systems lab. Today’s goal was to get our FPGA boards communicating with external sensor arrays. Cal Poly’s motto isn’t just marketing—we were literally soldering components and debugging logic gates within 15 minutes of class starting.

"The best part about the lab spaces here is that you're trusted with real hardware right from week one. If a circuit blows a fuse, you figure out why and fix it yourself."
🔬
Microcontroller Lab Bench

⏰ 01:15 PM 📍 1901 Marketplace

Lunch Break & Group Study

After wrapping up lab, I met up with a couple of classmates at the new 1901 Marketplace for lunch. Grabbed a bowl from the Mediterranean stand and snagged an outdoor table in the shade. We spent about an hour eating and reviewing our upcoming linear algebra midterm before afternoon classes.

⏰ 05:30 PM 📍 The Cal Poly "P" Trail

Golden Hour Sunset Hike

Once my final lecture ended at 5:00 PM, I met a few friends behind the dorms to hike up to the Cal Poly "P". It's a steep 15-minute climb, but the view of San Luis Obispo and Bishop Peak at golden hour makes it 100% worth it every single time. It's the ultimate way to decompress after a long day of coding and lectures.

⛰️

⏰ 08:00 PM 📍 Kennedy Library / Downtown SLO

Evening Wrap-up & Thursday Downtown

Wrapped up the evening with a quick two-hour study session at the library to stay ahead on project assignments. Since it's Thursday, a group of us ended the night heading downtown to check out the iconic Farmers' Market on Higuera Street for some fresh fruit and hot BBQ sliders.
