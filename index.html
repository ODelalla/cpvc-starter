🌸 07:15 AM 📍 Grand Avenue & Scout Coffee Co.

Starting Slow: Gentle Walk & Pink Matcha

Engineering coursework gets demanding fast if you wake up checking notifications. To protect my mental peace, I stick to a strict boundary: no email or phone screen for the first 30 minutes awake. I strolled down Grand Ave under the soft morning light, grabbed an iced strawberry matcha latte at Scout Coffee in the UU, and reflected on my goals for the day.

📷 Garrett grabbing his morning strawberry matcha at Scout Coffee
Peace Pillar #1: "Guard your first waking hour. Peace is created before the day demands your energy."
🌸 10:30 AM 📍 Bonderson Engineering Center

Mindful Focus: Computer Engineering Lab

In our hardware lab today, we worked on breadboarding circuit components and configuring microcontrollers. When code throws errors or circuits fail, it's easy to get frustrated. I protect my peace here by practicing mindful pauses—taking a deep breath, stepping back for 60 seconds, and treating debugging as a peaceful logic game rather than a crisis.

📷 Garrett staying calm & focused during microcontrollers lab
🌸 01:00 PM 📍 Leaning Pines Arboretum / Poly Canyon

Unplugged Midday Reset in Nature

Instead of eating in a crowded dining hall while staring at my phone, I walked over to the quiet gardens near Poly Canyon. Sitting under the eucalyptus trees with a fresh poke bowl and no screens gives my brain time to process and recharge. Nature is Cal Poly's best remedy for academic burnout!

📷 Garrett taking an unplugged lunch break surrounded by greenery
Peace Pillar #2: "Stepping outside for 30 minutes resets your nervous system faster than scrolling ever will."
🌸 05:15 PM 📍 The Cal Poly "P" Trail

Golden Hour Sunset Hike Above SLO

Physical movement is my favorite way to close out the school day. Once my last class wrapped up, a couple of close friends and I hiked up to the Cal Poly "P". Looking out over San Luis Obispo as the sky turns shades of soft pink and rose gold puts everything into perspective—reminding me that well-being always comes before grades.

📷 Garrett taking in the pink sunset from the top of the Cal Poly "P"
🌸 09:00 PM 📍 Evening Unwind & Cozy Space

Evening Digital Sunset & Warm Tea

By 9:00 PM, my laptop and study materials are put away. Protecting sleep quality is non-negotiable. I like to dim the room lights, light a lavender candle, read a chapter of fiction, and enjoy a warm cup of chamomile tea. Rest is essential productivity!

📷 Garrett unwinding with a book and tea before bed
Peace Pillar #3: "Rest isn't a reward for finishing your work; it's the foundation that makes your work possible."
